Risk Calculation Methodology & Definitions

Triple burden

High physical exposure, high social vulnerability, and high worker transition impact simultaneously (scores ≥ 6.5 across all three indices).

Double burden

Elevated risk (scores ≥ 6.5) across two of the three indexes.

Moderate risk

Elevated risk (scores ≥ 6.5) on one or fewer indices.

Low risk

Scores below 6.5 across all three indices, reflecting baseline exposure without acute structural compounding.

Read more about these calculations in the UNESCO report on pages 9, 17, and 51.
